About NourishMe

Why this app exists, and what it's built for.

NourishMe was created by Samarth Singh, a 9th grade student, as a project for the GIIS Hackathon 2K26.

The idea behind NourishMe came from a simple observation: healthy eating is confusing. There's plenty of advice online, but very little of it is actually personal — to your body, your goals, or what's already in your kitchen. NourishMe was built to fix that, turning generic guidance into a tool that works for the individual.

NourishMe does two things. First, it takes a person's details and goal and works out a personalised daily calorie target and macro breakdown. Second, users enter the ingredients they already have, and the app suggests meals they can actually make — complete with macros and filtered to their dietary needs.

The goal was to make healthy eating feel less like guesswork and more like something anyone can do, one meal at a time.
